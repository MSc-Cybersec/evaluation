## Bottom line

Static-analysis gating is proven by a reproducible chain of evidence showing that SAST runs automatically and prevents progression when a new Critical or High vulnerability is detected. Per **SAST-M-001**, the build **must fail** under those conditions; a scan report alone does not prove enforcement. Provide the auditor with the pipeline configuration, quality-gate policy, failed negative-test run, successful compliant run, and any formally approved deviations.

## Risk and impact

**Critical risk.** Without an enforced gate, exploitable code-level vulnerabilities—including injection and remote-code-execution flaws—may be merged or released despite being detected. This exposes applications, data, release integrity, and the organisation’s ability to demonstrate preventive SDLC control operation.
